## Favourite Series

**Naruto** is an anime about a young ninja who wants to become the hokage of the leaf village. However tragedies happen and in the end the Nine tails Fox was sealed inside him by his father, Naruto still strives to succeed. Alongside this, he forms bonds and gains knowledge on life and also perseverance.


### Why I like this series

- **Perseverance** – Naruto motivates me to persevere through tough situations.
- **Friendship** – As well as emphasising the value of friendship, this series also portrays the significance of trust and mutual aid.
- **Action/Combat** – With the various ninja skills that the ninjas have and the ensuing combat, this is a highly entertaining series.
- **Character Backstories** – All the characters have their own history and issues to overcome; this adds interest and allows people to relate to them.
